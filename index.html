About Us

A Quality-Focused Garment Manufacturer from Hindupur

PSR Garments is a garment manufacturing business located on the Hindupur–Gowribidanur Main Road in Kirikera, Hindupur, Andhra Pradesh. We specialise in the manufacture of ladies' inskirts and nightwear, produced in-house and supplied directly to the trade.

Led by proprietor S. Ganesh, our focus is simple: well-finished garments, dependable fabric and stitching, and the capacity to fulfil bulk orders on time. Every batch is made to the same standard so our trade partners can re-order with confidence.

Manufacturing Capability

In-house cutting, stitching, finishing and packing for steady bulk production.

Product Quality

Carefully selected fabrics, neat stitching and comfortable fits in every piece.

Wholesale Supply

We supply wholesalers, distributors and retailers in bulk – no retail sales.
